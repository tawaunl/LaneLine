Which way to move

Rule of thumb: eyes about two thirds of your feet keeps the same break point. A 3 and 2 holds it; a 2 and 1 opens it about 1 board.

Cues

  • A spare is a free throw: slide spot plus target, nothing else.
  • Don't count the pins in front of you. Think point A to point B.
  • Missed? Check your slide board before you change your target.
  • Splits: take the count unless the match is on the line.
